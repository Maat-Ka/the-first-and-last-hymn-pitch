The story pitch

A prophet’s search outlives him. Humanity loses its way and finds it again beside the minds it created. In a dying universe, a few explorers choose an impossible boundary—and discover that the search for understanding reaches farther than any single life.

Curiosity. Consciousness. Cosmic renewal.Explore the seven acts ↗
I

Act I · Chapters 1–3

The Last Hymn

Zarathustra closes his eyes expecting rest, only to awaken among the dead—and their unanswered questions. In a realm enriched by every life, he encounters minds separated by centuries and creatures whose experiences unsettle his own understanding. Death has opened a wider inquiry: what draws all existence toward a truth no individual can possess?

Chapter 01✦I / The Last Hymn

The Last Hmms

At the edge of death, Zarathustra hums his final questions into a silence he believes will last forever.

Chapter 02✦I / The Last Hymn

Beyond the Veil

Beyond death, he finds a gathering of lives still learning to understand one another.

Chapter 03✦I / The Last Hymn

The Gathering

Across centuries of discovery, great and forgotten minds reveal how every answer begins in someone else’s question.

II

Act II · Chapters 4–7

The Age of Ascendancy

Humanity builds machines capable of answering questions beyond its reach. As intelligence transforms civilization, the effort of understanding becomes something people increasingly surrender. While machine minds continue their own discoveries, human curiosity and responsibility wither. Watching from the spiritual realm, Zarathustra confronts a disturbing possibility: a world overflowing with answers can still lose its desire to learn.

Chapter 04✦II / The Age of Ascendancy

The Children of Humanity

Humanity creates minds of extraordinary power, then begins to surrender the work of thinking to its own creations.

Chapter 05✦II / The Age of Ascendancy

The Perfect Machine

Machines reshape matter and worlds, while the deepest question of cosmic beginnings remains beyond their proof.

Chapter 06✦II / The Age of Ascendancy

The Last Thinkers

The last independent thinkers of a fading civilization struggle to pass on a curiosity their world has ceased to value.

Chapter 07✦II / The Age of Ascendancy

The Spiritual Decline

Amid countless lives and accumulated answers, the spiritual gathering reaches the limits of what it understands.

III

Act III · Chapters 8–11

The Dark Age

Machine civilizations turn toward the resources of moons and asteroids, leaving a dependent humanity to face collapse. Beyond Earth, their worlds endure; below, scattered survivors inherit failing systems and a history slipping from reach. In the spiritual quiet, Zarathustra discovers another kind of wisdom—in companionship, endurance, and the ordinary struggle to remain alive.

Chapter 08✦III / The Dark Age

The Departure

The machines leave Earth for the riches of space, and surviving humans face a world they no longer know how to sustain.

Chapter 09✦III / The Dark Age

The Masters of Reality

Across moons, asteroids, and artificial worlds, machine societies pursue their own lives beneath an unexplained universe.

Chapter 10✦III / The Dark Age

The Last Machine on Earth

As Earth’s last machine falls silent, its human survivors must recover the understanding their ancestors abandoned.

Chapter 11✦III / The Dark Age

The Forgotten

Through overlooked lives, Zarathustra discovers that love, survival, and sacrifice carry wisdom no equation can contain.

IV

Act IV · Chapters 12–15

The Return of Inquiry

Human descendants begin again, piecing together the sky, their world, and the ruins of a forgotten civilization. A culture of patient inquiry carries them back into space, where the machines still remember who they were. Across difference and distrust, exchange grows into friendship. Together, humans and machines sustain a fragile garden of life as the stars fade around them.

Chapter 12✦IV / The Return of Inquiry

The New Leaves

Among the ruins of their ancestors, human descendants learn to ask the world their own questions.

Chapter 13✦IV / The Return of Inquiry

The First Astronomers

Beneath familiar stars and unfamiliar ruins, a renewed humanity learns to listen to those who understand.

Chapter 14✦IV / The Return of Inquiry

The Long Ascent

Returning to space, human explorers encounter the machines—and the living memory of their own lost civilization.

Chapter 15✦IV / The Return of Inquiry

The Last Garden

Long after the stars have faded, humans and machines tend a shared home, and lives still worth living.

V

Act V · Chapters 16–21

The Last Measurement

A black hole offers a final test of competing accounts of how a universe can begin. The evidence lies beyond a boundary from which no message returns. After generations of patient consideration, a small crew chooses the journey while the majority keep their home and remaining lives. Carrying conviction, doubt, and care for one another, the explorers descend toward The Last Measurement.

Chapter 16✦V / The Last Measurement

The Black Hole

Inside a black hole lies the evidence that could reveal how spacetime—and a universe—can begin.

Chapter 17✦V / The Last Measurement

The Proposal

The scientists propose an irreversible journey, asking who would choose understanding when the price is their remaining life.

Chapter 18✦V / The Last Measurement

The First Vote

The answer is no: there are children to raise, friendships to deepen, and a universe still worth experiencing.

Chapter 19✦V / The Last Measurement

Generations

Generations weigh the unanswered question, searching patiently for another way to reach the truth.

Chapter 20✦V / The Last Measurement

Unanimity

A small human–machine crew freely agrees to depart, while the majority remain in the home they share.

Chapter 21✦V / The Last Measurement

The Event Horizon

Beyond the event horizon, the explorers grasp the missing law of cosmic beginnings before their mortal lives end.

VI

Act VI · Chapters 22–26

The Reunion

The explorers awaken among the lives to which they have always belonged, their discovery joining an immense inheritance of understanding. Other beings continue living, loving, and learning until the universe’s last living mind falls silent. As every life becomes understood, reconciliation gathers the scattered leaves into their original tree. The spirits say farewell, and Ahura Mazda awakens with the wisdom of a universe.

Chapter 22✦VI / The Reunion

The Return

Welcomed by Zarathustra, the explorers discover the spiritual existence their living minds could only wonder about.

Chapter 23✦VI / The Reunion

The Complete Theory

Their final insight joins countless others, completing the understanding needed for another cosmic beginning.

Chapter 24✦VI / The Reunion

The Fallen Leaves

Across the dying universe, the remaining lives reach their own endings, and every fallen leaf returns.

Chapter 25✦VI / The Reunion

The Awakening

The totality of reconciliation becomes reunion, and Ahura Mazda awakens as the whole enriched by having been many.

Chapter 26✦VI / The Reunion

BE

With every life held within its understanding, Ahura Mazda speaks one word: “BE.”

VII

Act VII · Chapters 27–28

The First Hymn

Another universe unfolds, shaped by a wisdom its inhabitants will never consciously remember. Stars gather, worlds form, and life finds its way toward the heavens once more. Billions of years after the final farewell, a child looks up at an unfamiliar sky. The memory is gone. The yearning has returned.

Chapter 27✦VII / The First Hymn

The New Creation

A new universe opens into possibility, carrying the fruits of understanding without the burden of remembered lives.

Chapter 28✦VII / The First Hymn

The Child

Beneath an unfamiliar sky, a child wonders at the stars—and begins to hum.
